Backtest fidelity

16 of 64 resolved trades (25%) exited on the fill bar — the same H1 candle that filled the limit also hit SL. On H1 we cannot tell whether the 2R target or SL printed first, so these are booked SL (pessimistic, no look-ahead). Of the losers, 19 had the +2R price touched somewhere in the window yet booked a full −1R. Read the headline knowing this slice is H1-resolution-limited, not clean directional losses — a lower-timeframe fill model would reclassify some.
